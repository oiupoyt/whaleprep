What is stored, where it lives, and what you can do about it.

What is stored

Your name and email, the papers you build, your answers to each question, how long you spent on each one, and the score of every submitted paper.

Where it lives

Everything is written to a SQLite file on the machine running this service. Nothing is sent to a third party. There is no analytics script, no tracking pixel, and no external font request beyond the Google Fonts stylesheet used for typography.

What is never collected

No contacts, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising profile.

Who can read it

Anyone with access to the database file or to a signed-in browser session. Sessions are held in an HttpOnly cookie scoped to this site.

Deleting your data

Delete your account and every paper, answer and result attached to it by removing your row from the users table. Nothing is retained elsewhere, so that removal is complete.

Questions bank

Question content is study material held in the same database. It is not personal data and is not linked to you beyond the papers you build from it.
