This is a study tool built as a working copy of an existing practice site. Using it means you accept these terms.

What the service does

It builds practice papers from a bank of JEE Main style questions, runs them on a timed screen with +4 and −1 marking, and keeps your submitted papers so you can review results.

Your account

Sign-in is local to this installation. There is no password: anyone who knows the email can attach papers to that account. Do not use it for anything you would mind losing.

Question accuracy

Questions, answers and solutions are study material and may contain errors. Treat a wrong answer key as a bug, not a fact, and check anything you plan to rely on.

Acceptable use

No scraping at scale, no automated bulk paper generation, no attempts to reach other students' papers. Personal practice only.

The service is provided as is, with no promise that it will be available or error free.
